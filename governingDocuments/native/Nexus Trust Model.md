# Nexus Trust Model

## Purpose

Trust is established through evidence, verification, governance, and custody.

Trust is not established by AI output.

---

## Foundational Principles

NIM:

AI output is not completion.

The-Crucible:

Evidence is not proof.

Learning-Worker:

Extraction is not knowledge.

Crucible-Vetted-Learning-State:

Custody is not authority.

Vetting-and-Governance-oversite.:

Approval is not scientific proof.

---

## Trust Progression

UNVERIFIED

↓

EVIDENCE_AVAILABLE

↓

VERIFICATION_IN_PROGRESS

↓

VERIFIED

↓

GOVERNED

↓

CUSTODIED

---

## Rule

Trust progression must be evidence-backed and auditable.
